Biography

Jacqueline Fernandez

Sri Lankan actress and model based in India, known primarily for her work in Hindi cinema.

Born11 August 1985
BirthplaceManama, Bahrain
EducationUniversity of Sydney · Mass Communication
TitleMiss Universe Sri Lanka 2006
Acting debutAladin (2009)
Active2009–present

According to Wikipedia, Jacqueline Genevieve Fernandez was born in Manama, Bahrain, and raised there. She studied mass communication at the University of Sydney in Australia and later worked as a television reporter in Sri Lanka before entering modelling.

She won Miss Universe Sri Lanka in 2006 and represented Sri Lanka at Miss Universe 2006 in Los Angeles. In 2009, while in India for modelling work, she auditioned for Sujoy Ghosh’s fantasy drama Aladin, which became her acting debut and earned her the IIFA Award for Star Debut of the Year — Female.

Her breakthrough came with the psychological thriller Murder 2 (2011). Her later filmography includes commercially successful titles such as Housefull 2, Race 2, Kick, Housefull 3, Judwaa 2 and Vikrant Rona. Beyond acting, Wikipedia notes her work in television, brand endorsements, stage shows and humanitarian activities.
